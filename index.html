    Somatic Grounding & Anxiety Release

    Physical tension often locks in mental worry. Use these 3-to-5 minute somatic routines during panic spikes or racing thoughts.

    01

    Neck & Shoulder Tension Melt

    Ideal for upper body tightness caused by anxiety.

    • Seated Ear-to-Shoulder: Hold 30s per side with soft deep breaths.
    • Scapular Squeezes: Pull shoulder blades back for 5s, release (10 reps).
    ⏱ Duration: 3 Minutes
    02

    5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding

    Interrupt racing thoughts and overthinking loop.

    • Acknowledge 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste.
    ⏱ Duration: 5 Minutes
    03

    Spine & Hip Decompression

    Best for evening wind-down before restful sleep.

    • Legs Up the Wall: Recline with legs elevated on a wall for 3-5 mins.
    ⏱ Duration: 5 Minutes
